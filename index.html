The Legend in Your Hand

A comprehensive guide to the New Legendary Item System in Lord of the Rings Online.

Quick Answer: Can I Craft Them?

No. In the current system, you cannot craft the base Legendary Weapon or Class Item. You obtain them via quests or the Archives in Rivendell. However, crafting is vital for creating special upgrades called "Traceries."

Should I Bother With Crafting?

Yes! While you don't hammer out the sword itself, you need crafting for:

  • Word of Craft Traceries (Powerful stat bonuses)
  • Off-hand Weapons (LIs are Main-hand only)
  • Shields, Armor & Jewelry

The Power Gap: LI vs. Crafted

Why you should stop using standard weapons at Level 50.

Insight: Legendary Items scale linearly with your character level via "Reforging." Crafted weapons (Standard) have fixed stats and become obsolete every few levels.

Your Action Plan

1

Get your LI immediately

At Level 50 (or before entering Moria), complete the intro quest. Do not delay. This weapon replaces all drops.

2

Keep Crafting for "Word of Craft"

In the "Craft" slot of your LI, you can slot a crafted Tracery. These provide unique set bonuses (like Critical Rating or Mastery). This is the main link between crafting and LIs.

3

Reforge every 5-10 levels

Visit a Forge Master (in major hubs like Bree, Rivendell, Thorin's Hall) to "Reforge." This raises the item level and base DPS/Stats of the weapon instantly.

4

Don't ignore the Reward Track

Opened via the Menu (Shift+I). Playing the game earns XP for this track, which gives you the Ancient Script and Runes needed to upgrade your Traceries.
